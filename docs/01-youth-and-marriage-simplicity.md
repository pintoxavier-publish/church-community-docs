# Youth and Marriage Simplicity

A simpler marriage framework can help families avoid exploitative agents, reduce unnecessary expenses, and support couples with dignity.

## Key ideas
- Promote low-cost, transparent wedding pathways
- Limit unnecessary ceremonial expenses
- Support families with pastoral guidance and practical planning

## Why it matters
The goal is not to deny celebration, but to remove pressure, reduce burden, and help couples establish stable homes without debt and shame.
