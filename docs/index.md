# Community Empowerment Action Plan

This site collects the practical action plan for strengthening the Syro-Malabar community across health, youth, family stability, economic resilience, and digital governance.

## Core priorities
- Youth and marriage
- Family physician culture and health
- Media awareness and road safety
- Employment, technology, and AI
- Social, infrastructure, and governance reform
